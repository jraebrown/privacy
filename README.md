# privacy

## Personal Privacy Statement

I am committed to maintaining confidentiality in all personal and professional interactions. Any information shared with me in confidence will be treated as private and will not be disclosed without appropriate permission, unless required by law.

I use information technology responsibly by protecting access to devices, accounts, and systems through secure authentication and careful handling of digital information.

I follow data protection principles by collecting only necessary information, storing it securely, limiting access to authorized individuals, and retaining data only for as long as needed.
